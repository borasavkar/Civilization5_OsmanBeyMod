The civilization symbol (the tree of Osman's dream with a crescent) and the star pattern of the screens are drawn by `tools/build_art.py`.

## Map data

[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain): 1:50m land, lakes and geographic regions, 1:10m rivers and lake centrelines. GeoJSON version: [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector). Modern reservoirs (Lake Nasser, the Dnieper reservoirs, the Iraqi reservoirs) are left out because they did not exist in 1299.

## Game content

Unit models, building models and the base-game civilizations, leaders and city-states are Civilization V's own files; the mod refers to them by name and does not distribute them.

## Music

The five tracks in `Osman Bey/Sounds/` were supplied by the mod author.
